Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026. It applies to the Luzeira app for Windows, both the Microsoft Store version and the GitHub version.

In short: Luzeira doesn't collect any personal data, doesn't store any outside your PC and doesn't send any to anyone. It has no accounts, ads, telemetry or analytics.

What it reads and why

It reads the data from your PC that it shows on the USB display: CPU, graphics card and memory usage, network, disk, graphics card temperature and the time. It only draws them on your display, over the USB cable. They don't leave your computer.

What it stores on your PC

  • Your settings and your themes, in the Luzeira folder inside your Windows user data.
  • An activity log to track down bugs. It doesn't contain your user name or details about your network, and only you can see it.

When you uninstall it, you can delete all of it.

When it connects to the internet

  • Microsoft Store version: never. Updates are handled by the Store itself.
  • GitHub version: only if you turn on “Tell me about new versions”. It then checks the project's releases page on GitHub from time to time. It doesn't send anything about you or your PC; GitHub sees your IP address, as with any visit to a website.

The visual editor runs inside your PC: it uses a local server that only listens on your own computer (127.0.0.1) and can't be reached from the network.

What Luzeira doesn't control

Microsoft Store, Windows and the Microsoft WebView2 component used by the editor have their own privacy policies. Luzeira doesn't control or receive that data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, it will be updated here with its date. Luzeira is free software: you can review its code on GitHub. For any questions, open an issue on GitHub Issues.

Luzeira is free software under the GPL-3.0 license.

This website uses no cookies or analytics and doesn't load anything from other sites.
